An example you can try

Lower bills do not always mean a quick payback.

Suppose your current fridge’s label lists 190 units a year, and the fridge you want lists 118. At ₹7–10 per unit, the 72-unit difference is about ₹504–₹720 a year.

If the new fridge costs ₹25,000, electricity savings alone would take decades to cover it. Capacity, features and the condition of your current fridge may still matter.
