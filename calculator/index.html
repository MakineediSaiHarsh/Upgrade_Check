How to read your result

Break-even from electricity

Annual units saved = current label units − desired model label units. Divide net checkout price by the annual bill saving. If your current fridge must be replaced, compare running costs instead of treating this as a keep-versus-upgrade payback.

Our model search

Searches our Supabase refrigerator catalogue as you type. Coverage depends on the models added to the database, so a missing match is not evidence that a fridge is unavailable. Check every annual-unit figure against the exact label; provisional entries may need correction. Check the BEE source.

Why it is a scenario

Labels are standard-test values, not measurements of an ageing fridge at home. Electricity prices and purchase prices vary. Capacity, features, repair needs and warranty also matter. A long or impossible electricity payback can still be a useful answer.
